Common Questions

GOT QUESTIONS?
WE HAVE ANSWERS.

Will this void my car's factory warranty? +
No. AutoClutch Pro is 100% non-invasive. It only connects to the OBD-II port and clamps the existing pedal arm externally. No wires cut, no engine modifications. Factory warranty stays fully intact.
Which cars are compatible? +
Most manual cars made after 2010 with an OBD-II port — Maruti Dzire, Swift, Alto, Toyota Etios, Hyundai i20, Honda City, Tata Nexon manual, and hundreds more. Enter your car model at checkout and we verify compatibility before dispatch. Full refund if incompatible.
How long does installation take? +
2–3 hours. The kit includes an illustrated guide and step-by-step video. The Premium Kit (₹35,999) includes professional doorstep installation. We also have authorised service centres in Tier-1 cities.
What if the system loses power while driving? +
Failsafe design: the actuator clamp releases instantly on power loss and you immediately regain full manual clutch control. The system is engineered to fail safe, not fail active. Always.
Does Hill Start Assist really work? +
Yes. A built-in gyroscope/tilt sensor detects inclines and holds the clutch at the exact bite point until you press the accelerator. Zero rollback — perfect for Bengaluru flyovers, hilly terrain, and stop-and-go slopes.
Can I remove it? Is it reversible? +
Completely reversible in under 30 minutes. The clamp is mechanical (no adhesives), OBD-II plug pulls out, smart knob unscrews. Car returns to 100% stock. This is also how we preserve your warranty.
What payment options are available? +
UPI, net banking, credit/debit cards, Paytm/PhonePe, Cash on Delivery (select pincodes), and 0% EMI for 6 months via Bajaj Finance. Fleet operators can negotiate custom payment terms directly.
