🔌 Setup (one-time)

  • Download bridge.py and install.bat into the same folder
  • Double-click install.bat once
  • Come back here and click 🚀 Launch Bridge in the status bar
  • Click Start Camera — steering works!

💡 How It Works

The browser does face tracking via MediaPipe. It sends left/right/release commands over WebSocket to bridge.py, which presses real keyboard keys system-wide via pynput.

🎯 Tips for Accuracy

  • Face well-lit, no strong backlight
  • ~50–80 cm from camera
  • Sit upright, tilt naturally
  • Camera at eye level is best
